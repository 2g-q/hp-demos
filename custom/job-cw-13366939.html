About PEACE

Traditional roots, vintage soul, dressed for modern Tokyo.

We are not a rental counter or a souvenir shop. PEACE is a specialist store for genuine vintage kimono, yukata, haori and obi — alongside samurai armor, Noh & Kagura masks, kokeshi dolls and vintage bags. Many visitors from Europe and beyond find us while exploring Asakusa, and stay far longer than they planned.
